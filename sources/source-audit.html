YTD reconciliation boundary

The selected monthly market figures sum to 554,292 for 2026 and 493,386 for 2025. The October 2026 report gives official YTD totals of 554,081 and 493,427. Differences are −211 and +41 respectively. Public flash summaries do not identify the months behind those remaining cumulative revisions. We do not distribute the differences, infer monthly sales by subtracting successive YTD snapshots, or label the monthly sums as official YTD market totals.

Errata and limitations

  • March 2024: Nissan segment correction; no VW total correction stated. Official erratum
  • December/full-year 2024: two unnamed members and previous months revised; no month-by-month VW restatement supplied. Do not assume older VW figures are fully restated. Official erratum

Market shares combine original-month manufacturer counts with revised market denominators. They are not based on a fully restated manufacturer dataset. A complete latest-vintage monthly history would require naamsa’s underlying historical dataset. This audit verifies the evidence available in the public flash reports; it cannot establish that unpublished revisions did not occur.

No model-level, rental-channel or export figures enter these calculations. Growth = (current ÷ prior − 1) × 100. All values are vehicles, not revenue or profit.
