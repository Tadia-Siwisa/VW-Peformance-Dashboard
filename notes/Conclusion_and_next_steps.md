# Conclusion and next steps

## What this report establishes

Using the dashboard’s selected monthly counts, Volkswagen Group SA sold 54,915 vehicles in January–October 2026 versus 52,609 in the same months of 2025: growth of 4.4%. The market’s selected monthly sum grew 12.3%. Volkswagen exceeded market growth in April and May only. In October, its share declined from 11.1% to 10.5% year on year.

Volkswagen is selling more vehicles, but its growth is not keeping pace with the wider market. This warrants investigation into missed growth opportunities. It does not establish customer switching, a pricing problem or a causal explanation for any individual month.

## Recommended next investigation

Start with Polo Vivo and Polo. Compare similar alternatives on monthly finance payments using consistent deposit, term, interest and balloon assumptions, equipment and ownership costs. Use dealer enquiry and conversion data, stock availability and lost-sale reasons to distinguish affordability, availability and preference. These internal measures are proposed evidence to collect; they are not available in this public dataset.

Do not infer direct competition from total manufacturer sales alone. Keep model and buyer-channel comparisons separate from the aggregate manufacturer series.

## Does completion require a forecast?

No. The current deliverable is a descriptive performance report: it establishes what changed, compares that change with the market, explains the limits, and identifies a decision-relevant next investigation.

Forecasting is a separate study. Before adding it, define the decision it would support and assemble complete, consistently defined monthly history, including November and December. Compare a model with a seasonal baseline using held-out periods, report forecast uncertainty, and document revisions. A forecast made only to give the report a concluding number would not resolve the causes of the growth gap.
